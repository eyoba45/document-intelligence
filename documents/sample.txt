Adama Science and Technology University (ASTU) is a public university 
located in Adama, Ethiopia. It was established in 2011 and focuses on 
science, technology, engineering, and mathematics (STEM) education.

The university offers undergraduate and postgraduate programs in fields 
such as computer science, electrical engineering, mechanical engineering, 
and architecture. It currently has over 15,000 students enrolled.

ASTU has partnerships with several international universities and 
research institutions. The university aims to produce graduates who 
can contribute to Ethiopia's technological development and 
industrialization goals.

The main campus covers an area of 300 hectares and includes modern 
laboratories, a library, student dormitories, and sports facilities.
